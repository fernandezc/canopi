# Canopi 2.0.0

Canopi 2 makes the map the canvas. Every Design is drawn over a basemap, satellite imagery or your terrain data, and every object is stored where it is on Earth. Pan, zoom, turning the map and search move only the view. Desktop and Web share the canvas, panels, stories and PDF export; Desktop adds the full Plant catalog, the Data library and the Design notebook.

## What's new

### Moving around the map

- The map turns: Shift with right- or middle-drag (add Ctrl/Cmd for 15° steps), Shift ← and →, a trackpad or two-finger twist, or the compass's ring. N, Shift N or a click on the compass resets north; right-click a zone's edge for "Turn view to this edge". Plants, names and handles stay upright; notes, the grid and guides turn with the map.
- Right-drag, middle-drag or Space-drag pans in every tool; a still right-click opens the menu. Pinch and Ctrl + wheel zoom. Settings › Canvas › Pointing device chooses Mouse (the wheel zooms) or Trackpad (two fingers pan). The Pan tool (H) moved to the View menu.
- The map's controls sit in one group at the bottom right: scale bar, zoom out, the map scale (a menu of common scales), zoom in, Fit to Design, Show my location (Web) and the compass. Home or Ctrl 0 fits the Design.
- Touch: one finger selects or draws, two fingers pan, pinch and turn, a press and hold opens the menu. Handles grow to finger size after a touch. Help › Keyboard shortcuts (F1) lists the keys, mouse, pen and touch gestures.
- Show my location (Web Edition, on phones, tablets and computers) shows where you are and follows you; if the browser blocks it, the button says why. Your location is never saved in a Design or an export, and closing the Design turns it off.
- Reopening a Design returns to the view it was saved with; a Design appears once its map is drawn.

### Designing on the map

- A new Design opens with "Where is your site?": search a place or type coordinates and the map flies there. Fit to Design frames what the title bar, rails and panel leave.
- Backgrounds: OpenFreeMap styles (Liberty, Positron, Bright, Dark) labelled in your language, satellite imagery (free, or your Google Maps key kept on this device), or None; contour lines and hillshading are one switch away. If the map or Satellite with your key can't load, a notice says so with Retry; saving a corrected key in Settings › Map and imagery brings the imagery back.
- Every tool shows a card beside the tool rail with what to do next and its keys. Place plants previews the plant with its mature width and the distance to its nearest neighbour; Place a stamp previews its zones as placed and turns with [ and ].
- Plant a row follows a curved path: from a placed plant, click bend points (Alt: a sharp corner; the first point closes a loop). Drag a point to move it, drag or double-click a dot to insert one, Alt-click or Delete removes one. Choose an Interval or N plants; Enter, a double-click or the check button plants ordinary plants, left selected.
- Right-click or long press a zone corner or a row's point for Delete point, Sharp corner or Smooth corner; drag a zone's midpoint dot to insert a corner. While drawing a row, polygon or profile, Esc, Backspace or Ctrl Z removes the last point; a double-click or Finish shape ends a polygon.
- The selection toolbar is gone: right-click an object (or press the Menu key) for one menu in plain words (Cut, Copy, Paste, Duplicate, Select all of this species, Arrange, Save as stamp, Lock, Rotate…, Species details and more). The Edit menu and the palette enable what this menu enables.
- A chip at the bottom of the map names the selection (counts, spacing, a zone's area and perimeter). Zones can be named; an unnamed one is called by shape and size. A click inside a zone selects it.
- Rotate by a typed angle or in 15° steps; arrow keys nudge along the screen (10 cm, or 1 m with Ctrl/Cmd; with nothing selected they pan). Lock and unlock objects, or Unlock all; on a selection mixing both, Delete, Cut, Copy, Duplicate, Arrange and Lock act on the unlocked ones, while Group and Rotate need all unlocked. Shift keeps drawn edges on 45° to the screen.
- Plant symbols were redrawn: 33 one-colour symbols in four families, legible at small sizes.
- Plants in this Design › Display on the map colours plants by species, stratum or one colour, sets symbol size and outline, and labels codes or names (Shift L). Soften background dims busy imagery.

### Finding and placing plants

- The Plant catalog opens on recommended species (edible and multi-use first) and sorts by Name, Height or Edibility; quick filters sit under the search, the rest behind Filters as removable tokens.
- Species details lead with key facts (form, stratum, height, hardiness, light, soil, moisture, growth, uses). Photos name their source and licence. A species in your Design shows its code and count, with Select them and Zoom to them.
- Every plant list has the same finder (Ctrl F): names in every language, scientific names and codes, ignoring accents, capitals and small typos. Selected on map, Stratum and Form narrow any list; matches are ringed on the map.
- Place plants works before you pick a species: its card offers the Design's species, your Favorites, recent picks, a search and the full catalog. Drag a plant from the catalog or Favorites onto the map.
- A species with no name in your language shows its English name marked "(en)".

### Layers, data and analyses

- Layers lists the Design's layers, one Site data row whose eye shows or hides your terrain data, and Map: contour lines, hillshading and Background.
- Site data (Desktop, Ctrl 2) lists your terrain and height data, results under their source, in an order you drag. Rows show the value under the pointer; a click pins a point. Colours, range and opacity are saved with the Design; terrain types use your language's acronyms (MNT, DGM…).
- Profile charts each visible elevation or height layer along a line you draw: rise, steepest slope, Copy values.
- File › Import terrain or height data… takes single-band GeoTIFFs up to 2 GiB (such as IGN LiDAR HD) and places national grids (Lambert-93, Dutch, UK, Belgian, Swiss and others) where they belong; another coordinate system is refused in the dialog, naming it. It runs while you work; Cancel leaves nothing, a failure offers Retry.
- The Data library, opened from Site data or the File menu, keeps imported data on this computer for all Designs. Remove from Design never deletes data; Delete everywhere does.
- Analyze… calculates Slope (degrees or percent); Details show its source, whether it is out of date (Refresh) and Run again with changes.

### Planning panels

- Calendar: Range, One day or Unscheduled, a Completed switch, multi-line notes, and your language's first weekday. Add to calendar… creates an action for the selected plants or a zone.
- Budget: Set unit cost… from the right-click menu; Selected on map narrows the list; File › Export saves a CSV.
- Consortium assigns each species a stratum (Emergent, High, Mid, Low) and succession phases; the map's stratum colours and the Stratum filter follow it.

### Saved views, stories and printing

- View › Save current view… keeps where the map looks, its angle, background, layers, plant labels and focused species, with a picture; going to one moves only the camera and shows the same area in any window.
- The Stories panel builds a story from views: each step has a title, rich text and pictures (up to 1 MB each, 10 MB per Design). Present shows it full-window on desktop, web and phones beside the live map, with keys and swiping; presenting never changes the Design.
- File › Export › Planting plan (PDF)… opens a side sheet beside a live preview: paper, Map orientation (North up or As on screen), what to include, plant colours, field sheets and page thumbnails. Split into readable sheets cuts a page that would print too small; reference numbers match on every page.
- The plant key is grouped by habit, and Find in key (Ctrl F) rings a species' entry.

### Saving, settings and start

- Canopi saves a moment after every change and when you switch away or close. A new Design is a Draft until you Save as… a file. You are asked only when a save fails or the file changed outside Canopi; Revert to the version when opened… undoes a session.
- The Start screen shows Recent Designs with a sketch and counts, and your Drafts. A file that moved, was made by another version or is damaged says so. On short windows and landscape phones it scrolls as one page.
- Settings has Appearance, Map and imagery, Canvas, New Designs, Keyboard (single-key shortcuts can be turned off), Files and data and About. If the map stops drawing, Retry brings it back without losing the view, selection or Undo.
- Help › Command palette… (Ctrl Shift P) runs any command by name. F6 moves between title bar, tools, map and panel; screen readers announce tools and the map. On macOS shortcuts read Cmd.

### Web and phones

- The Web Edition keeps Designs in the browser (Download a copy to keep a file), imports and exports GeoJSON, exports PDF and Budget CSV, and presents stories.
- On phones the map fills the screen under a slim bar (Menu, Design name, Undo, place search); Select, Pan, Place plants and Polygon zone sit on a strip, panels in a bottom sheet. Only the zoom column (lens, zoom, Fit, location, compass) floats over the map; the armed tool, its options, the selection and notices sit in the sheet. Controls are at least 44 px.

### Languages

- Eleven languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Russian, Chinese, Japanese and Korean, with your language's number, price and date formats.

### Removed

- Rulers, ruler guides, Snap to guides and the "Locked · Unlock" chip; the grid and measurement guides remain.

## Before you upgrade

Canopi 2.0 does not open data from earlier versions, and deletes none of it.

- Designs from Canopi 1.x and from the 2.0 previews say "Made with Canopi before 2.0; Canopi 2.0 and later can’t open it" and are left unchanged. A hand-edited Design missing an id, a list Canopi writes or a saved view's area is refused, naming the field.
- Desktop: the 1.x Favorites, Recent Designs, Design notebook, saved stamps and settings are renamed `user.db.before-2.0-…` in the data folder; older Drafts stay where they are, unread. Web: the 1.x data and older Drafts stay in the browser, unread. Canopi says "Earlier data not opened" once and starts fresh. Data from a newer Canopi is refused and left untouched.
- Saved stamps from the earliest 2.0 previews say so and can only be deleted.
- If the Data library cannot be read as it is, Canopi rebuilds it from the files it keeps; items marked failed offer Retry. A library from a newer Canopi is left read-only.

## Known limits

- Switching from one open Design to another briefly shows an empty map, sometimes at the previous Design's view, before the new Design draws.
- On Linux with an NVIDIA graphics driver, the system may report that a WebKit process crashed when you quit Canopi. Your Design is saved before Canopi closes, so nothing is lost.
- On Linux, trackpad pinch does not zoom yet; use Ctrl + scroll.
- On the Web, the PDF plant key is one ungrouped list, because the Web catalog does not carry plant habits yet.
- The phone layout has not been checked on a real phone yet; full screen in Present is not offered on macOS.
- Hydrology, canopy analyses and story export come later. Performance with very dense Designs is not tuned.

## Source code

Canopi is AGPL-3.0 free software; its installers include the GeoLibre tool, which contains the AGPL-3.0-or-later `wbspatialstats` tools. The complete source of this release is Canopi at tag `v2.0.0` plus the `geolibre-rust` and `whitebox-wasm` revisions in `desktop/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, linked from the release page.
