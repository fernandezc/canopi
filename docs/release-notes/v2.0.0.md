# Canopi 2.0.0

Canopi 2 makes the map the canvas. Every Design is drawn over a basemap, satellite imagery or your terrain data, and every object is stored where it is on Earth. Pan, zoom and search move only the view. Desktop and Web share the canvas, panels, stories and PDF export; Desktop adds the full Plant catalog, the Data library and the Design notebook.

## What's new

### Designing on the map

- A new Design opens with "Where is your site?": search a place or type coordinates and the map flies there. Fit to Design frames what the title bar, rails and panel leave, even a thin band.
- Backgrounds: OpenFreeMap styles (Liberty, Positron, Bright, Dark) labelled in your language, satellite imagery (free, or your Google Maps key kept on this device), or None; contour lines and hillshading are one switch away.
- Every tool shows a card beside the tool rail with what to do next and its keys. Place plants previews the plant with its mature width and the distance to its nearest neighbour; Place a stamp turns with [ and ].
- Plant a row follows a curved path: from a placed plant, click bend points (Alt: a sharp corner; the first point closes a loop). While drawing, drag a point to move it, drag or double-click a dot to insert one, Alt-click or Delete removes one. Choose an Interval or N plants; Enter, a double-click or the check button plants ordinary plants, left selected.
- Right-click or long press a zone corner or a row's point for Delete point, Sharp corner or Smooth corner. While drawing a row, polygon or profile, Esc or Backspace removes the last point.
- The selection toolbar is gone: right-click an object (or press the Menu key) for one menu in plain words (Cut, Copy, Paste, Duplicate, Select all of this species, Arrange, Lock, Rotate…, Species details and more).
- A chip at the bottom of the map names the selection (counts, spacing, a zone's area and perimeter). Zones can be named; an unnamed one is called by shape and size.
- Rotate by a typed angle or in 15° steps, nudge with the arrow keys (10 cm, or 1 m with Shift; with nothing selected they pan the map), lock and unlock objects, Unlock all in one step; Shift keeps polygon edges on 45° angles.
- Plant symbols were redrawn: 33 one-colour symbols in four families, legible at small sizes.
- Plants in this Design › Display on the map colours plants by species, stratum or one colour, sets symbol size and outline, and labels codes or names. Soften background dims busy imagery.
- Text and labels stay readable in the dark theme and over satellite.

### Finding and placing plants

- The Plant catalog opens on recommended species (edible and multi-use first) and sorts by Name, Height or Edibility; quick filters sit under the search, the rest behind Filters as removable tokens.
- Species details lead with key facts (form, stratum, height, hardiness, light, soil, moisture, growth, uses). Photos name their source and licence. A species already in your Design shows its code and count, with Select them and Zoom to them.
- Every plant list has the same finder (Ctrl F): names in every language, scientific names and codes, ignoring accents, capitals and small typos. Selected on map, Stratum and Form narrow any list; matches are ringed on the map.
- Place plants works before you pick a species: its card offers the species in the Design, your Favorites, recent picks, a search and the full catalog.
- A species with no name in your language shows its English name marked "(en)", in lists, details and the PDF.

### Layers, data and analyses

- Layers lists the Design's layers, one Site data row whose eye shows or hides all your terrain data, and Map: contour lines, hillshading and Background (Satellite, Street map, None) with its settings under it.
- Site data (Desktop, Ctrl 2) lists your terrain and height data, results under their source, in an order you drag. Rows show the value under the pointer; a click on empty ground pins a point. Colors, range (Cut outliers included) and opacity are saved with the Design.
- Profile charts each visible elevation or height layer along a line you draw: rise, steepest slope, Copy values.
- File › Import terrain or height data… takes single-band GeoTIFFs (such as IGN LiDAR HD), says if they cover your site and runs while you work. Cancel leaves nothing of a first import; a failed import offers Retry or Dismiss.
- The Data library, opened from Site data or the File menu, keeps imported data on this computer for all Designs. Remove from Design never deletes data; Delete everywhere does.
- Analyze… calculates Slope (degrees or percent) from ground elevation; Details show its source, whether it is out of date (Refresh) and Run again with changes.

### Planning panels

- Calendar: Range, One day or Unscheduled in one control, a Completed switch, multi-line notes, and a week that starts on your language's first day. Add to calendar… creates an action for the selected plants or a zone.
- Budget: Set unit cost… from the right-click menu; Selected on map narrows the list; File › Export saves a CSV.
- Consortium assigns each species a stratum (Emergent, High, Mid, Low) and succession phases; the map's stratum colours and the Stratum filter follow it.

### Saved views and stories

- View › Save current view… keeps where the map looks, with its background, layers, labels and focused species, in the Design; going to one moves only the camera.
- The Stories panel builds a story from views: each step has a title, text with bold, italic, bullets and links, and pictures (up to 1 MB each, 10 MB per Design). Drag steps or use Alt ↑ and Alt ↓; every change has Undo.
- Present shows a story full-window on desktop, web and phones: the step's text and pictures beside the live map, step dots, Previous and Next, keys and swiping. Presenting never changes the Design.

### Printing

- File › Export › Planting plan (PDF)… opens one side sheet beside a live preview: paper, what to include, plant colours (As in the Design, Grayscale or Black), field sheets and page thumbnails.
- Add field sheet draws a detail page over any part of the Design; Split into readable sheets cuts a page that would print too small. Drag or arrow keys move a page.
- The plant key is grouped by habit (Tree, Shrub, Herbaceous, Climber, Other), and Find in key (Ctrl F) jumps to a species with its entry ringed.

### Saving, settings and start

- Canopi saves a moment after every change and when you switch away or close. A new Design is a Draft until you Save as… a file. You are asked only when a save fails or the file changed outside Canopi; Revert to the version when opened… undoes a session.
- The Start screen shows Recent Designs with a sketch and counts, and your Drafts. A file that moved, was made by another version or is damaged says so.
- Settings has Appearance, Map and imagery, Canvas (Pointing device: Mouse or Trackpad), New Designs, Keyboard (single-key shortcuts can be turned off), Files and data and About.
- Help › Command palette… (Ctrl Shift P) runs any command by name. Menus, dialogs and panels fit small windows; F6 moves between title bar, tools, map and panel, and screen readers announce tools and the map.

### Web and phones

- The Web Edition keeps Designs in the browser (Download a copy to keep a file), imports and exports GeoJSON, exports PDF and Budget CSV, and presents stories.
- On phones, the Web Edition switches to a phone layout: the map fills the screen under a slim bar (Menu, Design name, Undo, place search); Select, Pan, Place plants and Polygon zone on a strip; panels in a bottom sheet (Layers, Plants, Catalog, More) that opens to half or the whole screen. Controls are at least 44 px and fields do not zoom the page.
- On phones the tool card, the selection and map notices share one bar at the top of the sheet, and the zoom column holds the inspection button; nothing else covers the map, and the pinned-names legend is hidden.

### Languages

- Eleven languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Russian, Chinese, Japanese and Korean. Counts, prices and dates follow your language's formats; the calendar week starts on its first day.

## Before you upgrade

Canopi 2.0 does not carry data over from earlier versions, and deletes none of it.

- Rows planted along a path are ordinary plants; the `.canopi` format gains nothing for them.
- Designs from Canopi 1.x and from the 2.0 previews say "Made with Canopi before 2.0; Canopi 2.0 and later can’t open it" and are left unchanged.
- From Canopi 1.x, Favorites, Recent Designs, the Design notebook, saved stamps, settings and Drafts move aside unchanged on first start (`user.db.before-2.0-…`, `drafts.before-2.0-…` in the data folder). Recent 2.0 previews keep all but older-format Drafts. On the Web they move to a `before-2.0` browser key, or, when the browser is full, stay hidden until you delete Drafts and reload. Canopi says so once. Data from a newer Canopi is refused and left untouched.
- Saved stamps from the earliest 2.0 previews stay in the list but can no longer be placed.
- If the Data library cannot be read as it is, Canopi rebuilds it from the files it keeps and says so; items marked failed come back with Retry. A library saved by a newer Canopi is left untouched and read-only.

## Known limits

- Importing terrain data needs no extra install (no GDAL); the GeoLibre tool for Slope ships in the installers.
- On the Web, the PDF plant key is one ungrouped list, because the Web catalog does not carry plant habits yet.
- The phone layout (narrow, or short landscape windows) has not been checked on a real phone yet; full screen in Present is not offered on macOS.
- Hydrology, canopy analyses and story export come later.
- Performance with very dense Designs is not tuned.

## Source code

Canopi is AGPL-3.0 free software; its installers include the GeoLibre tool, which contains the AGPL-3.0-or-later `wbspatialstats` tools. The complete source of this release is Canopi at tag `v2.0.0` plus the `geolibre-rust` and `whitebox-wasm` revisions in `desktop/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, linked from the release page.
